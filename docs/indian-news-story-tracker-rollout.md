# Indian publisher feeds and Story Tracker rollout

Production schema status (2026-09-29): `2026-09-source-attribution-and-digest.sql`, `2026-09-indian-sources-story-tracker.sql`, `2026-09-admin-added-feeds.sql`, and `2026-09-story-tracker-ai-summaries.sql` are applied to the configured Hostinger MySQL database. Do not rerun them there. A private pre-migration schema and feed-settings backup is at `/private/tmp/belgaum-migration-backup-20260928.json` on the migration workstation.

Admin-added RSS feeds are trusted without a second approval step. A feed must be active, hosted on its publisher's domain, and return direct links on that domain to ingest. Google News, Reuters, FeedBurner, excluded publisher domains, and World feeds are not eligible. GPT-6 Luna classifies each feed item for India relevance; uncertain or foreign-only reports stay hidden. Legacy articles can be restored when their direct URL matches a configured active publisher domain and category and Luna confirms India relevance.

To initialize another database, apply the four migrations above in order. Configure Indian publisher RSS feeds in Admin → RSS Feeds, then run the RSS fetch job. The scheduler also retries pending classification and builds Story Tracker timelines. If using external cron without the in-process scheduler, call `POST /api/cron/story-tracker` with `Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>` after RSS fetching. The daily digest uses the same public articles and event timelines.

To recover recent articles written before feed attribution, run `node scripts/restore-legacy-news.mjs --days=7 --per-category=60` first for a dry run, then add `--apply`. The script only publishes direct configured publisher URLs that Luna judges India-related. Use `--local-only --days=14 --per-category=150` to recover Belagavi articles in older India feeds. Run `--link-only` first as a dry run, then with `--apply`, to connect exact RSS item URLs from the four latest completed fetches to their public article IDs. The Hostinger recovery on 2026-09-29 produced 246 public articles, including 17 Belagavi articles, and repaired 411 RSS item links.

AI News appears next to Entertainment and shows developing stories with at least two reports and a publisher, AI trending, or reader-interest signal. Opening a story shows its timeline and the saved GPT-6 Luna summary. Summaries stay within 75 words and link to the publisher reports used. The summary cache refreshes when a new report joins the story.

Verify the homepage, category pages, search, story timelines, feed.xml, and daily digest with a current India-focused item and a foreign-only item from the same feed. The foreign-only item must not appear. If the OpenAI API is unavailable, new items stay pending and hidden until classification succeeds.
